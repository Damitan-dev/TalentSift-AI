Current limitations

How to interpret these numbers

This MVP view compares interview language, not protected demographic characteristics.

Small sample sizes can produce unstable averages. Average-score differences should not be interpreted as causal evidence.

Recruiter overrides are excluded from the average AI score so that this page measures the AI evaluation output separately from later human decisions.

Controlled guardrail, matched-pair, and voice-pipeline tests should be reviewed separately alongside production monitoring statistics.
